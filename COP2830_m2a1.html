Cybersecurity Basics: Staying safe Online

By Edward Pena de Dios

Sept 6, 2026

Introduction

Cybersecurity affects almost everyone, even those who do not work with computers or technology. People of all ages and backgrounds use the internet every day to check email, shop online, use social media, pay bills, and send texts and funny content to family and friends. While some of these things may seem harmless, they can pose significant risks to your personal information and online security. Learning a few basic cybersecurity practices can help everyday users recognize threats and make safer choices online. This page covers some common threats and simple steps anyone can take to protect their devices, accounts, and personal information.

Why Cybersecurity Matters?

Think about how much of your personal life is connected to the internet. Your email, your social media, photos in cloud storage, passwords, your residential address, and financial data. All of this is valuable to cybercriminals, they can use it to steal your identity, access your bank accounts, make purchases under your name, scam your friends and family, etc. Information that may seem unimportant on its own can become useful when combined. Understanding how this can be used against you shows why staying safe online is so important.

Common threats

Cyber threats can show up in many of the things we do every day. One of the most common threats is phishing, where someone may send a text or email claiming there is a problem with a package delivery, asking you to reset your password, or pretending to be a friend or family member asking for money. The goal is usually to trick you into clicking a fake link that may take you to a website designed to look legitimate, where you may unknowingly give away passwords, financial information, or other personal information. Some websites and downloads can also contain malicious software designed to steal information or harm your device. Even a password you have used for years can become a risk if it is exposed in a data breach and you use that same password on other accounts. These situations may look normal at first, which is why recognizing when something does not seem right is an important part of staying safe online.

How do I protect myself?

Staying safe online does not have to be complicated or require a lot of technical knowledge. One of the best things you can do is slow down and pay attention to what you are being asked to do. Many scams are designed to create a sense of urgency by telling you that a package cannot be delivered, your account will be locked, a payment has failed, or you need to take action immediately. Before clicking anything, look at who sent the message and whether it makes sense for you to be receiving it. If a message claims to be from your bank, a store, or another company you use, avoid using the link in the message and instead open the company's official website or app yourself. This gives you a safer way to find out if there is actually a problem with your account.

Protecting your accounts is just as important. Using a different password for each account prevents one stolen password from giving someone access to several parts of your online life. Multi-factor authentication can provide additional protection by requiring you to verify your identity in another way before someone can sign in. Keeping your phone, computer, browser, and other applications updated also helps fix security problems that could otherwise be used against you. Above all, trust yourself when something online does not feel right. A strange email address, a website that looks slightly different than usual, or a request for information you normally would not be asked to provide are all good reasons to stop and verify what you are seeing. Taking a few extra moments before clicking, downloading, sending money, or sharing personal information can be one of the simplest and most effective ways to protect yourself online.

Learn more

Knowing how to protect yourself online is something that becomes easier with time and awareness. The more familiar you become with the ways scammers try to get your attention or personal information, the easier it can be to notice when something does not seem right. Since these methods continue to change, it is important to keep learning about new threats and how they affect people. The FTC Consumer Advice website is a great place to learn about current scams and find more information on what you can do to stay safe online.
